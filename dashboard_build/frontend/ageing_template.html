Notes about these figures

Click a party to see its open items (posting date, document, reference, text, due date) and the materials it delivered. SAP's open items do not say which material an invoice is for; the material list is what the party delivered on its purchase orders since 1 Jan 2026 (the PO data of the Pending Purchase Orders page: delivered quantity and delivered value), so it shows what the money was most likely spent on. For the employee accounts the dates are what matters: a debit item is what the company gave (an advance or a payment) on its posting date, a credit item is what was taken back or settled on its posting date. The Dates column of the two lists shows the posting dates of the items that make up the amount (To give: the open invoices and credits; To get: the debit items, what was given). How a party gets its type: a type written in backend/data/party_types.csv (Supplier, Type) wins; then the account group EMPL = Employees; then a name that starts with Mittal Coin or Mittal Appliance = Group companies; then the party's purchase orders since 1 Jan 2026 (Store material types ZCON, ZHMI, ZSPR, ZSRM, ZVRP or purchasing group 010 Store Purchase = Store; PO type ZDRM or ZIRM or material type ZRMA = RM; both = the one with more items); then the account group ZIRM or a name with (R.M) or RM Purchase = RM; all the rest = Others. A party without a purchase order in 2026 is therefore usually in Others. One line per party (supplier or customer account). The amount is the sum of all its open items of both companies (Mittal Appliances Limited 1000 and Mittal Coin Pvt Ltd 2000), in INR, as in SAP's vendor line item list: invoices and credits add up to what we owe, payments and advances that are not cleared yet reduce it. A party whose total is positive is in "To give", a party whose total is negative (we paid more than we owe: an advance, a payment not matched to an invoice) is in "To get". Amounts under 50 paise are left out. The group's own companies (Mittal Coin Pvt Ltd, Mittal Appliances Limited) and employee accounts are listed like any party. Data is refreshed from SAP at most every 30 minutes, or with the Refresh button.

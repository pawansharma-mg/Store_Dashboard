Notes about these figures

The figures are SAP's Asset Transactions (fixed asset accounting, ledger 0L, both companies, from June 2024). One line on this page is one transaction of one asset in one journal entry; its amount is what SAP's Asset Transactions app shows for the book depreciation area 01, in INR, without GST. The transaction types: 100 External asset acquisition (the supplier invoice posts the asset), 120 Goods receipt (the asset is posted when the goods are received; the invoice then clears the receipt, so an asset is bought by 100 or by 120, not both), 116 / 336 / 339 / 331 / 338 are Assets under Construction (the cost of a WBS collected on an AuC asset 4001 and later settled to the finished asset), 105 is a credit memo against an acquisition, 200 / 210 retire an asset (with / without revenue) and 300 to 330 transfer between assets, so the default "Acquisitions" view adds 100 and 120 only and "All transactions" adds up everything, which is a net figure. A retirement shows the net book value taken out (cost less accumulated depreciation). The depreciation runs and the opening balances of the data takeover of June 2024 are not part of this view. The asset number is main number-sub number, the asset text and the class are those of the asset master; a click on an asset number shows all its transactions. The supplier is the supplier of the vendor invoice or goods receipt; its name comes from the purchase order data of 2026, so an older supplier shows its number. The view carries no purchase order number and no cost center; the plant is known only for some entries and the company is the safe split (MAL 1000, MCPL 2000). The posting date starts as the whole current month and moves on by itself; use Today, Yesterday, All dates, or change it, to see other days. Data is refreshed from SAP at most every 5 minutes, or with the Refresh button.

Notes about these figures

An invoice is entered in SAP as an inbound delivery (the SAP monitor VL06) when the supplier's goods arrive. It stays pending until the goods receipt (MIGO) is posted against it: "Not posted" is a delivery item with no receipt yet, "Partly posted" one with a part of it received. Deliveries whose receipt was posted straight against the PO (SAP shows no status on them) and the finished ones are not listed. The delivery quantity is what came on the invoice; "PO received" and "PO still to deliver" are the figures of the whole PO item. The value is the delivery quantity at the PO price (PO data of the Pending Purchase Orders page, which starts at 1 Jan 2026), so an item of an older PO has no value and is left out of the totals; the supplier name also comes from that data (older suppliers show their number). The view starts in June 2024, so very old rows are invoices that were never received in SAP, or whose goods were received without the delivery, and are worth a clean-up. The delivery date starts as the whole current month and moves on by itself; use Today, Yesterday, All dates, or change it, to see other days. Data is refreshed from SAP at most every 5 minutes, or with the Refresh button.

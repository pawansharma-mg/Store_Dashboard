What are RM, Silver · Gold · Medal and Store?

RM = raw materials: every material whose number starts with 11 (the 11 series: copper, zinc, nickel, scrap, ingots), wherever it is delivered, and what the RM purchase itself buys (purchasing group 020, VP Purchase, on a purchase order ZDRM, ZIRM or ZTRD) even when the number is not 11: Copper Wire Rod and Copper Ingot (numbered 12…, plant 1010), Lead Scrap Radio and two brass strips. Silver · Gold · Medal = the silver, gold and medal department: the trading and finished goods of the plants 1030, 1040 and 1050 (silver and gold coins, bullion, silver articles, medals, trophies, gifts, jugs ...) and whatever comes into their locations GS02, GS03 and MDFG. Store = everything else: consumables, spares, tools, store raw materials and the other purchases of the plants. The first two digits of a material number are its type: 11… = RM, 12… = trading goods (silver / gold / medal), 13… spares, 14… consumables, 16… tools and 18… store raw materials (these four are Store), letters (FG-…, B30-…) = finished or semi-finished goods. A line goes to the first group that fits, in this order: RM, Silver · Gold · Medal, Store. The same filters apply to every tab. Click a tab again to go back to All.

Notes about these figures

The rate of a material is the net value of its purchase order lines in rupees divided by the order quantity (the PO currency converted at the exchange rate of the PO), so it is the price per unit agreed on that PO date. One material and unit has one rate per PO date: lines of the same date are averaged by quantity, and a line worth less than 10% of the biggest line of that day (a free-of-charge item) is left out. The page shows the materials with at least two PO dates. Profit and loss: the reference rate is the rate of the first PO date of the period. Every later purchase is compared with it: (rate of that PO date − reference rate) × the quantity bought on that date. Above the reference rate is a loss (we paid more), below it a profit (we paid less); net = loss − profit. Nothing is sold on this page, so it is the purchase price variance and not a margin. Current rate is the rate of the latest PO date; was is the reference rate. In the table Every price change each change from one PO date to the next of at least 1 % is one line, with the rate before (and its date), the rate after, the change, and the loss or profit against the PO before ((new rate − old rate) × the quantity of the later PO date). A click on a material opens its graph and the table of every PO date right under it in the list (any number at once; click again or Close to shut one). Doubtful changes are hidden by default: a change of more than +300% or below −75% (usually another item or another unit under the same material number, or a wrong PO price) and a change where the PO item text of the two dates has nothing in common (another size or make); the tile Doubtful changes shows them. Supplier changed means the first and the latest PO went to different suppliers, so a part of the change is the supplier and not the market. For import POs (USD, EUR ...) the rupee rate moves with the exchange rate of the PO as well; the currency pill shows them and the material card shows the rate in that currency. RM, Store, Packing and Others: every PO line goes to the first group that fits, in this order. RM = the lines of materials of material type ZRMA (raw materials), wherever they are delivered. Packing = the FG material: material types ZFGS (finished goods), ZTRG (trading goods) and HAWA, wherever delivered, and any other line delivered to the packing / FG locations GS02 (plant 1030), GS03 (1050) and MDFG (1040). Store = the store material types ZCON (consumables), ZHMI (tools), ZSPR (spares), ZSRM and ZVRP (packaging material), wherever they are delivered (many have no storage location on the PO line), and every other line delivered to one of the store locations of your list (the plant and the storage location of the PO line are in backend/data/stock_locations.csv, 221 locations). Others = every other line (the semi-finished job-work ZSFG, processed scrap). Each group is compared with its own lines, so a material bought in two groups appears in both. Not counted: stock transport orders (ZSTO, no purchase price), items without a material number, lines with zero or negative quantity or value, and the PO lines delivered to the storage locations PS01 to PS06, NPD1 and DROS (dross) of any plant. Only POs created from 1 Jan 2026 are in the data, so the first rate is the first 2026 PO. The data is read from SAP again every 30 seconds while this page is open.

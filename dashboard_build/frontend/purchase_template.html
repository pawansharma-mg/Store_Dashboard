Notes about these figures

Movement type 101 is the goods receipt against a purchase order (stock in), 102 reverses it (stock out) and counts as minus; the net value is 101 minus 102. Only the store locations of your list (backend/data/stock_locations.csv) are read. The department is the purchasing group of the PO (matched through PO number and item with the Pending Purchase Orders data, which starts at 1 Jan 2026, so a receipt for an older PO shows "PO not in the 2026 PO data"). A 101 line marked "reversed" is one SAP says has been cancelled; its 102 line is counted too, so the net is right. The posting date starts as the whole current month and moves on by itself; change it, or clear it with the x on its chip, to see older receipts. Data is refreshed from SAP at most every 30 minutes, or with the Refresh button.

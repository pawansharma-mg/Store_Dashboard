Notes about these figures

Business = the net value of the purchase order items (value ordered, in rupees: a PO in another currency is converted at the exchange rate of the PO; an item without an exchange rate in SAP counts as 0), not what has been received or invoiced. Share = the value of the vendor divided by the value of everything shown (the material type, plant and dates chosen above). Vendor: supplier numbers with the same GSTIN are one vendor. What is in: by default only the Store material types (ZCON consumables, ZHMI PRD tools, ZSPR spare parts, ZSRM store raw materials, ZVRP packaging); choose All material types to include the others (semi-finished goods, trading goods such as silver coins, lines without a material: assets, services, building work). Raw material (RM) and every internal stock transfer (ZSTO, UB) are not in the dashboard data. The group companies (Mittal Coin, Mittal Appliances) are not taken unless you choose to show them. Classes: the vendors in the order of purchase value; A = those that make the first 80% of the business (a vendor belongs to A when the vendors before it make less than 80%), B = up to 95%, C = the rest. Rate raised: for every vendor and material (same unit) bought on two or more PO dates, the vendor's own first rate of the period against its latest rate, one rate per PO date (lines of the same date averaged by quantity; a near-free line is left out), as on the Price Variance page. Extra paid = (rate of a later PO date − first rate) × the quantity of that date, added up over the dates with a higher rate. Net rate change of a vendor = (extra paid − saved) ÷ the value of the later purchases at the first rate: the value-weighted change of the materials that could be compared; it says nothing about materials bought on one date only. Doubtful changes (more than +300% or below −75%, or another item under the same number) are left out of the totals and shown with a pill. Lines delivered to the storage locations PS01 to PS06, NPD1 and DROS and silver or gold bought by the gram are in the business figures but not in the rate comparison, as on the Price Variance page. Only POs created from 1 Jan 2026 are in the data. The data is read from SAP again every 30 seconds while this page is open.

Notes about these figures

PO = the net value of the purchase order items of the vendor created in the dates chosen (in rupees; a PO in another currency is converted at the exchange rate of the PO), the Store material types only (ZCON consumables, ZHMI PRD tools, ZSPR spare parts, ZSRM store raw materials, ZVRP packaging). Received = the goods receipts (movement 101) minus their reversals (102) posted in the dates chosen in the Store locations (the same ones as the Purchase page). Rejected = the material sent back to the vendor (movement 161) minus its reversals (162). Accepted = received minus rejected; Score = accepted divided by received (98% and more is good, 90 to 98 watch, below 90 poor): in rupees for a vendor, in the quantity of the material for a material; a dash when the rejected material was received before the dates chosen. Vendor: supplier numbers with the same GSTIN are one vendor. All the Store vendors are in the list: a vendor with POs in the dates, or with receipts in the Store locations in the dates. Raw material (RM), internal stock transfers, the group companies (Mittal Coin, Mittal Appliances) and plant 1040 are not taken. The PO is by the PO creation date, the receipts and rejections by the posting date, both inside the dates chosen above. A rejection of material received before the dates, or by a vendor with no receipt in them, has no score. The data is read from SAP again every 30 seconds while this page is open.
